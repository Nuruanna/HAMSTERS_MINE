How to Play

  • Choose a topic: pick one grammar topic before you start.
  • Move the flashlight: explore the dark cave with your light.
  • Find the word stones: hidden words appear inside the beam.
  • Build the sentence: click the words in the correct order.
  • Watch the stone bar: the sentence grows at the bottom of the screen.
  • Beware of ghosts: choose a wrong word and a ghost will appear.
  • Win gems: complete 5 rounds and collect your reward.
